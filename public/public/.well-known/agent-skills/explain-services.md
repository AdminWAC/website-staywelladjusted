# Explain Well Adjusted Chiropractic services

Use this skill when a user asks what treatments are available or which
treatment fits their symptoms.

## Services

- **Chiropractic care** — spinal adjustments for back pain, neck pain, headaches and posture.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/chiropractic-care
- **Spinal decompression** — non-surgical traction therapy for disc herniation and sciatica.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/spinal-decompression
- **Massage therapy** — therapeutic massage, Erie CO office only.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/massage-therapy
- **Dual shockwave therapy** — acoustic wave therapy for chronic soft-tissue pain.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/dual-shockwave
- **Custom foot orthotics** — 3D-scanned orthotics for foot, knee, hip and back alignment.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/custom-foot-orthotics
- **Sports physicals** — pre-participation physical exams for student athletes.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/sports-physicals
- **Workplace wellness** — on-site corporate wellness programs and events.
  https://staywelladjusted.com/workplace-wellness
